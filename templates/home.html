CONNECT4
How to play

Two players. One winner.

Four in a row.
Game on.

Challenge a friend to Connect Four. Take turns dropping your pieces and find your way to four before they do.

Let’s play

Grab a friend and pick your color.

Simple rules. Plenty of strategy.

  1. Take turns. One player uses red pieces and the other uses yellow.
  2. Choose a column. Your piece drops into its lowest available space.
  3. Connect four. Make a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of four pieces in your color to win.

If the board fills up without a winner, it’s a draw.
